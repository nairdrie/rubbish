RUBBISH - playtest build {VERSION}
Built {BUILT}. Thanks for helping test!

BEFORE YOU START
- Open Steam and sign in. The game uses Steam to find your friends.
- Steam will say you're "playing Spacewar". That's expected: Spacewar is
  Steam's free test app, and we borrow it until Rubbish has its own page.

PLAY
1. Unzip the whole folder somewhere easy, like your Desktop.
   Don't run it from inside the zip.
2. Double-click {EXE}.
3. "Windows protected your PC"? The game isn't code-signed yet.
   Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
4. If Windows Firewall asks, allow it on Private networks.
5. "Rubbish needs Steam"? Open Steam, sign in, then click Try again.
6. Alt+Enter switches between fullscreen and a window.

PLAYING TOGETHER
- One person clicks "Host with Friends" on the main menu.
- Everyone else starts the game first, then joins in one of these ways:
  a) Click "Join a friend" on the main menu: friends who are hosting
     show up with a JOIN button. Or right-click the host in your
     Steam Friends list > Join Game.
  b) Accept the host's Steam invite (the host presses F2 to send one).
  c) The host finds their invite code in Esc > Invite friends (click
     Show). Click "Join a friend", open the Code tab, type it, click Join.
- Up to 4 people per world. Esc opens the menu (Invite, Settings,
  Unstuck, Leave / Quit). Solo pauses; with friends the world keeps going.
  Unstuck on foot calls YOUR truck to you (a drone flies it over; $25).
  Stuck inside a wall? It moves you out first, then calls the truck.
- Everyone needs the same build. Check the date in BuildInfo.txt.

WHAT'S IN THIS BUILD
Everyone runs their own garbage business, next door to each other on
Depot Row, each in their own colour with their own truck. When you join,
pick one of your saved businesses or start a new one. Rename yours at
the laptop desk in your shop.
- Trash is shared: grab a bag and it's gone for everyone. Whoever's
  truck it lands in gets paid.
- Go anywhere: any street, any shop. You can look around other shops
  but can't touch their trash.
- Hire each other from the Payroll app on your laptop (hire and fire
  are laptop-only). Employees drive the boss's truck and sort in the
  boss's shop for an hourly wage. Quit any time from Esc.
- Sell and trade valuables with each other on ebae (Neighbours tab).
- Chaos: right mouse = shove / swing, E = pass, snatch, pick up a
  flopped friend or hitch a ride on the back of any truck, Space = get
  up / wriggle free / hop off, Z = play dead. Grab the same bag as
  someone and it's a tug of war: pull on the beat, mashing rips it.
Your own save is safe: your business goes home with you when you leave.

SOMETHING BROKE?
1. Press Windows+R, paste this line, and press Enter:
   %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Archway Games\Rubbish
2. Send Player.log to whoever sent you the game. Also send
   Player-prev.log if you restarted the game after the problem.
3. Say roughly when it happened and what you were doing.
The logs include your Steam name but no passwords.

HELP TUNE THE GAME
The game keeps a private play log (how long things take, where you got
stuck, what you bought). It never leaves your computer by itself and has
no names in it. After a play session, zip the "metrics" folder inside
the same folder as above (...\Archway Games\Rubbish\metrics) and send it
along. To turn it off, start the game with -nometrics.
